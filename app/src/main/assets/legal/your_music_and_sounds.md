# Your music & sounds

## The music you make is yours

You own the songs you create in SANWOLF DAW. We don't take a cut, need credit or claim any rights in your tracks.

## The sounds are royalty-free for your songs

You can use every sound that comes with the app in your own music, for free, forever:

- **SANWOLF sounds**: the drum kits, one-shots, loops and synth presets made by SANWOLF.
- **VCSL instruments**: the sampled piano, electric piano, marimba, vibraphone, kalimba, balafon and harp from the Versilian Community Sample Library, released into the public domain (CC0 1.0).

This covers commercial use, including:

- selling your songs and releasing them on streaming services;
- releasing through distributors (DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby and others);
- using them in videos, games, podcasts, ads and film or TV (sync);
- playing them live and on the radio.

You don't need to credit us, though a shout-out is always appreciated.

## The one rule: don't resell the raw sounds

The sounds are for making music, not for repackaging. Please don't:

- share or sell the bundled sounds on their own, for example as a sample pack, kit, preset bank or sound library (changed or unchanged);
- register the raw sounds with Content ID or a similar system, or claim them as your own standalone samples. This stops other SANWOLF users being hit with false claims.

Registering **your finished song** with Content ID or a distributor is fine.

## Sounds you bring in

Anything you record or import (vocals, samples, loops) is covered by its own rights. Make sure you're allowed to use it.

Full details of where each sound comes from are in Settings › About › Sound licences.
